# Surface Brief: Quảng Ngãi Tourism Atlas (`/`)

## Visitor Mode
Persuade & Explore — first-time visitors immediately see the diversity of Quảng Ngãi (Lý Sơn sea islands, Sa Huỳnh coastal heritage, Măng Đen & Chư Mom Ray highlands), search or filter destinations, and enter the interactive map or itinerary builder.

## Audience & Task
Domestic and international travelers planning routes or looking up destinations on desktop and mobile. Primary actions: Search destinations (`HeroSearch`), Open interactive map (`/map`), or Build an itinerary (`/itinerary`).
